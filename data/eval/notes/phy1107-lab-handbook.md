# PHY-1107 Laboratory Handbook (fictional course, for the answer eval)

## Conventions for this module

- In PHY-1107 labs, take g = 10 m/s² in every calculation unless the sheet says otherwise. Don't use 9.81: the
  marking scheme is built around 10.
- Report every measured result to 3 significant figures.
- Quote the uncertainty of a stopwatch time as ±0.2 s, for human reaction time, not the 0.01 s the display shows.

## Submitting reports

- A lab report is due 9 days after the session, by 17:30.
- Submit it through Labwise, the department's lab portal; reports sent by email aren't marked.
- Late reports lose 7% of the mark per day, up to a cap of 35%.

## The experiments

- **Kater pendulum.** The string is 0.85 m long and the bob is a 120 g brass cylinder. Safety goggles are required,
  because the bob can come loose.
- **Cooling curve.** The water starts at 82 °C and is read every 30 s for 20 minutes. Goggles aren't needed for this
  one; heat-resistant gloves are.
