# Weeknight garlic and chilli pasta

Serves 2. Takes about 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 200 g spaghetti
- 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 red chilli, finely chopped (or 1 tsp chilli flakes)
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- Small bunch of flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- Zest and juice of half a lemon
- Salt and black pepper
- Parmesan to serve (optional)

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti
   until just al dente. Reserve a cup of the pasta water before draining.
2. While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a wide pan over low heat.
   Add the garlic and chilli and cook gently for 3-4 minutes until the
   garlic is golden but not brown.
3. Add the drained pasta to the pan with a splash of the reserved water.
   Toss over medium heat until the sauce clings to the pasta.
4. Off the heat, stir in the parsley, lemon zest and juice. Season well.
5. Serve with grated parmesan.

## Notes

Double the garlic if you like. Anchovies melted into the oil at step 2 add
a lot of depth.
