# Travel checklist

Things I always forget, so they live here.

## Before leaving

- Passport (check expiry is more than 6 months out)
- Travel insurance confirmation saved offline
- Tell the bank about travel dates
- Download offline maps for the destination
- Charge the power bank

## Packing

- Universal plug adapter
- Noise-cancelling headphones
- A spare pair of glasses
- Medication for the full trip plus three days
- One outfit more than the number of days, no more

## Day of travel

- Water the plants
- Turn off the water heater
- Empty the fridge of anything that will go off
- Leave a spare key with the neighbour
